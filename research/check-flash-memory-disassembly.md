# FireRed CheckForFlashMemory disassembly

`bpr-j-rev0-retail` is the Japanese origin reference. All 8 cataloged releases (Japanese, English, German, French, Italian, Spanish) independently resolve one boot-call target with the 38-byte flash-detection CFG. The two calls are named from the pinned `pret/pokefirered` source at `037335f4c725d7c9aecdac87066f2002b4bd7e14`. Maps publish addresses and hashes, not ROM bytes or instruction halfwords.
